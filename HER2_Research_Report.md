
# HER2 Protein Sequence Analysis

## 1. Research Objective
To analyze the human HER2 (ERBB2) protein sequence
using Python and publicly available UniProt data.

## 2. Data Source
Database: UniProt
Accession ID: P04626
Protein ID: sp|P04626|ERBB2_HUMAN
Source URL: https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04626.fasta

## 3. Methods
- Downloaded the HER2 protein FASTA sequence.
- Calculated sequence length.
- Calculated theoretical molecular weight.
- Counted amino acid occurrences.
- Calculated amino acid percentages.
- Generated an amino acid composition bar graph.

## 4. Results
- Total amino acids: 1255
- Molecular weight: 137909.068 Da
- Molecular weight: 137.909 kDa

## 5. Scientific Interpretation
The sequence contains 1255 amino acid residues.
The calculated molecular weight is a theoretical estimate
based on the primary protein sequence.

Amino acid composition describes how frequently each
amino acid occurs in the sequence.

## 6. Limitations
- This is a computational sequence analysis.
- The calculated molecular weight does not account for
  post-translational modifications such as glycosylation.
- This analysis does not demonstrate drug effectiveness
  or predict breast cancer treatment outcomes.
- Further structural and experimental validation is required.

## 7. Conclusion
The human HER2 protein sequence was successfully retrieved
and analyzed using Python and Biopython.

This project demonstrates a basic bioinformatics workflow
that can support further computational drug discovery studies.
